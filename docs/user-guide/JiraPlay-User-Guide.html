What you can do with JiraPlay

Your team's Jira board, turned into a game. Finish work, earn XP, level up.

Screenshots show JiraPlay's built-in demo team

JiraPlay shows your team's Jira Cloud issues as a game. Every teammate is a hero with a level, an XP bar and hearts, and their issues are their quests. It's still your real Jira: every status change, comment, reassignment and new issue you make in JiraPlay happens in Jira straight away.

Use it inside VS Code, next to your code, or in your web browser.

Get connected in two minutes

A step-by-step guide walks you through connecting your Jira account: your Jira site, your email and an API token, with a button that opens Atlassian's token page. JiraPlay checks everything with Jira before saving. Until you connect, you can explore with a demo team.

See your whole team at a glance

  • Sprint banner: the sprint's name, dates, days left, goal and how much of it is done.
  • Hero cards: each teammate's level, XP, hearts and open issues, with overdue and hardest work first.
  • Party list: jump to any teammate. YOU marks you, and Unassigned collects issues nobody owns yet.
  • New work stands out: a teammate's card glows when they pick up a new issue.
  • Keyboard friendly: ← → to switch teammates, Enter to open, Esc to go back.

Each teammate's board, just like Jira

Open any teammate to see their issues in the same columns as your Jira board, for example To Do, In Progress, Code Review and Done. JiraPlay reads the columns from Jira, so they stay in step when your board changes.

Everything about an issue in one place

  • See the assignee, status, type, priority, story points, due date and description.
  • Difficulty stars show how big the issue is, and Reward shows the XP it's worth.
  • Read and add comments. Press ⌘/Ctrl + Enter to post.
  • Open in Jira whenever you need the full Jira page.

Move work forward

  • Change status: pick the next step from the moves your Jira workflow allows.
  • Mark done with one click on the issue's icon.
  • Reassign by dragging an issue onto another teammate, or onto Unassigned.
  • Create issues right from a teammate's card.
  • Completed issues are clearly marked with a green ✓ and the XP they earned.

Earn XP and level up

You getHow
XP10 XP per story point for every finished issue, or 10 XP if it has no points.
LevelsLevel up as XP adds up: level 2 at 10 XP, 3 at 40, 4 at 90, 5 at 160, and so on.
HeartsEveryone has 4 hearts and loses one for each overdue issue.
Difficulty stars1 to 5 stars from story points, or from priority when there are no points.
Sprint progressThe whole team's XP and how much of the sprint is done, shown at the top.

Your personal dashboard

Click your picture in the top right to open your profile: your level and XP, plus a dashboard of your work.

  • Counts of your assigned, to do, in progress, done and overdue issues, and your percentage done.
  • Filter by clicking a count, or by one of your board's columns.
  • Search by title, or type an issue key to jump straight to it.
  • All my issues goes beyond the sprint to everything assigned to you anywhere in Jira.

Works where you work

In VS Code

  • JiraPlay icon in the left sidebar to open your board.
  • Your level in the status bar, e.g. ⚔ LVL 3 · 7 open, updated even when the board is closed.
  • Level-up notifications when you reach a new level.
  • Your own Jira account: every change is made as you.

In your browser

  • The same board at http://localhost:5173, running on your own computer.

Safe with your Jira account

  • Your API token is only ever sent to your own Jira site.
  • In VS Code it's kept in your system keychain, and Sign out of Jira removes it.
  • JiraPlay has no server of its own and collects no data.

Get started

  1. VS Code: in the Extensions view, open the … menu, choose Install from VSIX… and pick the JiraPlay file. Web app: run npm install and npm run dev in the JiraPlay folder, then open http://localhost:5173.
  2. Follow the Connect Jira guide that opens.
  3. Finish an issue and watch your XP grow.
