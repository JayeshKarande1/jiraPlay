## Finish issues, beat the boss

- **XP** is 10 × story points for each finished issue. It's remembered after issues leave the board, and taken away if an issue is reopened.
- **The sprint boss** has one HP per XP of open work. Every finished issue is a hit.
- **Streaks** count days in a row with finished work; weekends don't break them.
- **Achievements** and **classes** are earned from what you actually do: fix bugs and you become the Rogue.

Marking an issue done waits five seconds with an **Undo** button (or ⌘/Ctrl+Z) before it's sent to Jira. The sidebar shows your level, streak and open issues.
