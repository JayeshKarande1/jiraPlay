## Your team as a party

Every teammate is a hero card with a **level**, an **XP bar** and **hearts** that drop for overdue work. Their Jira issues are listed on the card.

- Click a hero, or use **← →** and **Enter**, to see their issues in your Jira board's columns.
- Drag an issue onto another hero to reassign it, or use the **Assign** menu in the issue.
- The board starts on demo data, so you can look around before connecting anything.
